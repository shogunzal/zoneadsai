THE DETAILS MATTER

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Is ZoneAds a SaaS platform or commercial service for external clients?

No. ZoneAds is not a commercial SaaS platform, agency, or public software service. We are an independent direct-response advertiser. This workspace is our private, internal media-buying suite built and operated exclusively in-house by ZoneAds to manage our own TikTok advertising campaigns.

What does ZoneAds actually do for our in-house desk?

ZoneAds brings our TikTok creative testing queue, campaign launches, stop-loss rules, CBO scaling workflows, account connections, and conversion P&L into one unified internal console. We define the creatives, budgets, and rules; ZoneAds executes the repeatable API tasks across our owned accounts.

Who owns the TikTok ad accounts and pays for media spend?

ZoneAds is the direct advertiser. We own and operate our connected TikTok Ads Manager accounts, authorize our own budgets, and pay TikTok directly for all advertising spend. We do not sell ad accounts or manage third-party client funds.

What specific TikTok Marketing API permissions and endpoints are used?

We request only the minimum Marketing API scopes required for our internal operations: Ad Account Management (/advertiser/info/), Ads Management (/campaign/create/, /adgroup/create/, /adgroup/status/update/), Creative Management (/file/video/ad/upload/, /tt_video/info/), and Reporting (/report/integrated/get/).

Do we keep human control over every campaign and budget?

Yes. Every campaign launch, budget allocation, and scaling threshold is configured and supervised by our in-house media-buying desk. Automated stop-loss rules only pause underperforming ad sets within explicit pre-approved guardrails.

How are credentials secured and how can access or data be deleted?

Account connections use TikTok’s official OAuth 2.0 flow—we never collect or store TikTok passwords. Access tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256. Authorization can be revoked anytime directly in TikTok Ads Manager, or by emailing support@zoneadsai.com for immediate token and data deletion.

Can external users or third-party advertisers sign up?

No. There is no public sign-up, customer onboarding, or external account registration. Access is restricted strictly to authenticated internal media-buying operations.
