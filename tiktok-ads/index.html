Before connecting

A few things worth knowing.

Can I connect a TikTok advertiser account?

Eligible signed-in team operators can connect an advertiser account after the administrator completes the approved app configuration. TikTok still controls advertiser eligibility, permissions, ad review and delivery.

Who owns the ad account and controls spending?

The direct advertiser does. ZoneAds does not provide or sell ad accounts. You set and authorize budgets, remain responsible for platform charges, and decide whether to enable any campaign changes. The service acts only within the permissions and instructions you give it. TikTok bills the advertiser directly.

What connection and permissions are used?

The advertiser connection uses TikTok’s official OAuth flow without sharing your TikTok password with ZoneAds. The workspace uses granted permissions strictly to manage campaign, ad-group, ad and creative settings. Unrelated accounts and private messages are outside this workflow.

How can I revoke access or delete platform data?

You can revoke ZoneAds authorization through TikTok’s account controls at any time. You can also contact privacy@zoneadsai.com to request disconnection or deletion of stored TikTok platform data. Revocation stops future API access but does not automatically pause ads already running on TikTok.

Are ad approval or campaign results guaranteed?

No. TikTok controls its own account eligibility, ad review and delivery. Performance depends on creative, audience, offer and other factors. ZoneAds does not guarantee approval, revenue or return on ad spend. TikTok is a third-party brand used here to identify the integration; ZoneAds is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or an official partner of TikTok.
