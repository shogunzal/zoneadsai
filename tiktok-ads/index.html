Why We Use the TikTok Marketing API

How this internal workspace interacts with TikTok APIs.

Our personal dashboard integrates with official TikTok endpoints to replace manual repetitive tasks, enforce daily spend stop-loss safeguards, and manage creative rotation across our owned accounts.

01 / CAMPAIGN MANAGEMENT

Direct Campaign Deployment

We use the /campaign/create/ and /adgroup/create/ APIs to structure auction campaigns, set CBO daily budgets, and configure targeting for our direct-to-consumer offers under operator confirmation.

02 / CREATIVE & SPARK ADS

Video Upload & Spark Authorization

We upload vertical video assets created by our in-house team directly via the /file/video/ad/upload/ endpoint and link authorized creator Spark Ads authorization codes to our campaign ad drafts.

03 / REPORTING RECONCILIATION

Automated Spend & Metric Sync

The dashboard queries the /report/integrated/get/ endpoint hourly to pull real-time impressions, clicks, and spend data, reconciling them with our landing page conversions without manual CSV downloads.

04 / STOP-LOSS SAFEGUARD

Operator-Enforced Spend Caps

Our workspace monitors ad delivery against pre-set CPA thresholds. If an ad set exceeds our target acquisition cost, our media buyer is alerted to pause or reallocate budget, preventing wasted ad spend.

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct Advertiser Verification FAQ

Is ZoneAds a SaaS platform or commercial tool for external clients?

No. ZoneAds is not a SaaS platform, commercial software, or an agency providing services to third parties. We are an independent performance marketing and direct advertising group. This workspace is our private, internal media-buying console built and operated solely by Faisal Yahaya to manage our own direct-to-consumer advertising campaigns.

Who owns the TikTok advertising accounts and pays for ad spend?

ZoneAds is the direct advertiser. We own and operate our TikTok Ads Manager accounts, set our own campaign budgets, and pay TikTok directly for all advertising charges. We do not manage client ad accounts, resell accounts, or manage external media budgets.

What specific TikTok Marketing API permissions are requested?

We request access only to core marketing endpoints: Ad Account Management (to identify our connected accounts), Ads Management (to draft campaigns and ad groups), Creative Management (to upload our video assets), and Reporting (to pull hourly spend and performance metrics). We do not request social, messaging, or financial administration permissions.

Can external users or clients sign up for an account?

No. There is no external registration, client onboarding, subscription pricing, or public access. The workspace is restricted strictly to our internal media-buying operations.

How are credentials and data stored?

OAuth tokens are stored securely on our private backend, encrypted at rest using AES-256. We never collect or handle TikTok account passwords. You can revoke authorization at any time directly through TikTok Ads Manager, which immediately stops all API calls.
