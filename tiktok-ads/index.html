The integration

A clear route from authorization to campaign management.

ZoneAds is designed for businesses and operators using advertiser accounts they own or have permission to manage. TikTok remains responsible for account eligibility, available settings, ad review and delivery.

01 / AUTHORIZE

Connect through TikTok

The advertiser uses TikTok’s authorization experience, selects eligible accounts and grants only the presented permissions. ZoneAds never requests a TikTok password.

02 / PREPARE

Build a complete draft

Select the account, objective, creative, destination and supported campaign settings inside one structured workflow.

03 / REVIEW

Confirm before action

The operator sees the budget, destination and creative together before any campaign-creation request is sent.

04 / MANAGE

Stay in control

Review current campaigns and update supported status or budget settings within the permissions TikTok grants.

Before connecting

Important questions, answered plainly.

Can I connect a TikTok advertiser account today?

Eligible users will be able to connect an advertiser account after TikTok approves the application and ZoneAds completes the production configuration. TikTok controls advertiser eligibility, permissions, ad review and delivery.

Who owns the advertiser account and controls spending?

The advertiser does. ZoneAds does not provide or sell accounts. The user selects an account they own or are authorized to manage, sets the budget and confirms requested actions. TikTok bills the advertiser directly.

Does ZoneAds guarantee account or ad approval?

No. TikTok controls advertiser eligibility, permissions, ad review, delivery and platform availability. ZoneAds does not guarantee approval, campaign delivery, revenue or return on ad spend.

How can an advertiser revoke access?

The advertiser can revoke authorization through TikTok’s controls. They can also contact ZoneAds support to request disconnection or deletion of stored TikTok platform data. Revocation stops future API access but does not automatically pause live ads.

Is ZoneAds affiliated with TikTok?

No. ZoneAds AI is independent software and is not an official TikTok partner, endorsed service or part of TikTok. The TikTok name is used only to identify the third-party platform integration.

Need integration or privacy support?

Contact ZoneAds about advertiser authorization, requested permissions, disconnection or deletion of connected-platform data.
