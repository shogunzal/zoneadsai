Frequently Asked Questions

Clarifications on our direct advertiser setup.

Is ZoneAds a SaaS platform or commercial service for other businesses?

No. ZoneAds is not a SaaS platform, public software tool, or marketing agency for hire. We are an independent performance marketing group that runs advertising for our own e-commerce and consumer brand campaigns. This workspace is our internal operational console used strictly by our own media-buying team.

Who owns the advertising accounts and controls ad spending?

ZoneAds is the direct advertiser. We own and operate our TikTok Ads Manager accounts, set our own campaign budgets, and pay TikTok directly for all advertising costs. We do not provide, sell, or rent advertising accounts to third parties.

What TikTok Marketing API permissions are used?

We use the official TikTok Marketing API strictly to manage our own campaigns, ad groups, video creatives, and performance reporting under direct operator supervision. We do not request unrelated social, messaging, or financial administration scopes.

Can external advertisers sign up or use this workspace?

No. There is no external registration, client onboarding, or public software access. The workspace is restricted strictly to authenticated ZoneAds media buyers.

Is ZoneAds affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok?

No. ZoneAds is an independent performance marketing business. It is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or an official partner of TikTok or ByteDance. The TikTok name is used strictly to identify the advertising platform integration.
