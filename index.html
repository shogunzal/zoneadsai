Access and data controls

Your accounts. Your authorization. Your decision.

ZoneAds is designed to work only with accounts an advertiser explicitly authorizes. It does not ask for platform passwords, sell accounts, guarantee approvals or override platform review.

AUTHORIZATION

Platform-controlled connection

Eligible advertisers authorize access through the advertising platform’s own flow and select the accounts they want to connect.

MINIMUM ACCESS

Purpose-limited permissions

Permissions are limited to the campaign, creative and reporting features actually presented in the product.

REVOCATION

Withdraw access

Users can revoke platform authorization and separately request deletion of stored connected-platform data.
