Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Does ZoneAds AI manage or run ads on my behalf?
No. ZoneAds AI is an automated productivity software and workflow tool for media buyers. You launch ads from your own ad accounts with your own budget, and you control all targeting, spend, and creative decisions.
How does the TikTok API integration work?
ZoneAds AI connects to your TikTok Ad Accounts via TikTok's official Commercial API using OAuth 2.0. Once authorized, the platform automates repetitive campaign creation tasks, uploads creative assets, and retrieves performance statistics for your private dashboard.
Whose payment methods and ad accounts are used?
Yours. ZoneAds AI never processes ad spend payments or charges commissions on your ad spend. All billing for ad delivery occurs directly between you and TikTok Ads.
Can I track revenue from my affiliate trackers?
Yes. ZoneAds AI supports standard Server-to-Server (S2S) postbacks from trackers like Voluum, RedTrack, BeMob, Keitaro, as well as direct webhook payloads from custom e-commerce checkout platforms.
How do I request early access?
ZoneAds AI is currently in early private beta for verified media buyers and agencies. Click "Request Access" to submit your application or message us directly via Telegram or Discord.
