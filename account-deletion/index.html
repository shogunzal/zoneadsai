Account & Platform Data Deletion

Instructions for disconnecting connected advertising platforms or requesting deletion of stored platform tokens and data.

Last updated: 28 September 2026 · Privacy contact: privacy@zoneadsai.com

How to make a data deletion request

Email the privacy team

Write to privacy@zoneadsai.com from the email associated with your verified account. Use the subject line “Data deletion request.”

Specify the scope

Identify whether you wish to disconnect a specific platform integration (e.g. TikTok Ads), purge stored access tokens, or remove account correspondence records.

Verification & Execution

Upon verifying authority over the account, our team purges the requested platform tokens and data from our active records within 48 hours.

Revoking TikTok Access Directly

You may also revoke ZoneAds authorization directly through your TikTok account settings at any time. Revocation instantly invalidates access tokens and stops all future API requests.

Public Website Data

The public website provides information on our direct advertising operations and does not store or process third-party advertising credentials.
