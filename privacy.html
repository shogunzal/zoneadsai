Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last Updated: September 11, 2026

1. Introduction

ZoneAds AI (operated at zoneadsai.com, "we", "our", or "us") provides a specialized bulk ad management, asset organization, and performance monitoring software platform for advertisers and digital media buyers.

This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website, apply for access, and connect your third-party advertising accounts (including TikTok for Business / TikTok Ads Manager) through our service.

2. TikTok Developer API Data Use & Compliance

ZoneAds AI integrates with the official TikTok Commercial API via OAuth 2.0. In strict accordance with the TikTok Developer Terms of Service and Commercial API Policies:

  • Data Collection Scope: When you connect your TikTok Ads account, we only request API access tokens and basic ad account metadata (such as Advertiser IDs, Campaign/Ad Group IDs, creative status, spend, impressions, clicks, and conversions) necessary to provide campaign creation and reporting features.
  • Purpose Limitation: TikTok API data is used solely to allow you to create, edit, upload, duplicate, and monitor ad campaigns directly within your authorized accounts.
  • No Sale or Redistribution: We never sell, lease, transfer, or distribute your TikTok API data or account information to any third party, data broker, or advertising network.
  • No Cross-Account Data Leakage: Your ad data, performance metrics, and creative assets remain completely isolated to your authenticated account.
  • Token Security: TikTok OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored securely in encrypted form using AES-256 encryption. We never have access to your personal TikTok account passwords.

3. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account & Contact Information: Name, work email address, company name, messaging handles (Telegram/Discord), and basic business profile submitted during onboarding.
  • Uploaded Creative Assets: Video files, ad copy text, thumbnails, and Spark Ads authorization codes that you choose to store or publish through ZoneAds AI.
  • Attribution Data: Server-to-Server (S2S) postback identifiers, revenue figures, and conversion counts sent to your private reporting endpoint.
  • Technical Logs: IP address, browser user agent, access timestamps, and diagnostic error logs for system performance and security auditing.

4. How We Protect Your Data

We implement rigorous technical and organizational security measures, including:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / SSL) encryption for all web and API traffic.
  • AES-256 at-rest encryption for sensitive access tokens and credentials.
  • Strict role-based access controls and automated vulnerability monitoring.

5. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

You retain complete ownership and control over your data:

  • Revoking Access: You can disconnect your TikTok Ads account at any time either directly in your TikTok Developer portal or from your ZoneAds AI settings. Disconnecting instantly invalidates your active OAuth tokens.
  • Data Deletion Requests: You may request complete erasure of your account, uploaded assets, and stored performance logs at any time by emailing our data protection team at privacy@zoneadsai.com. We process all deletion requests within 30 days.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We may utilize trusted cloud hosting infrastructure providers (such as AWS, Cloudflare, or Vercel) strictly to operate and secure our server instances. These providers are bound by strict confidentiality and data processing agreements.
