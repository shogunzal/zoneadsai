Legal and Data Controls

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ZoneAds AI handles information across our website, direct performance advertising operations, and connected advertising-platform integrations.

Effective date: 28 September 2026 · Contact: privacy@zoneadsai.com

1. About this policy

ZoneAds AI (“ZoneAds,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is an independent performance marketing and direct-response advertising group. We build and deploy media campaigns for our e-commerce, consumer app, and brand partner offers. This policy applies to our public website, internal campaign workspaces, support channels, and connected advertising-platform integrations (collectively, the “Services”).

2. Information we collect

In our advertising operations, we may process:

  • Contact information: name, work email address, and direct correspondence when you contact support.
  • Campaign and creative information: ad drafts, creative assets, vertical videos, Spark Ads authorization codes, landing page URLs, and daily budget parameters.
  • Attribution and performance metrics: landing page impressions, click counts, conversion postbacks, CPA, and aggregated platform performance.
  • Connected platform data: advertiser IDs, authorized campaign structures, creative references, and access tokens required to operate enabled integrations.
  • Technical data: IP address, device headers, timestamps, and security logs collected strictly for infrastructure integrity.

3. How we use information

  • Execute and supervise direct-response advertising campaigns.
  • Enforce human-in-the-loop spend controls and daily budget limits.
  • Reconcile platform metrics with first-party attribution records.
  • Respond to support, data access, and deletion requests.
  • Maintain operational security and comply with legal requirements.

We do not sell personal information or connected advertising platform data to any third party. We do not use connected platform data to train general-purpose artificial intelligence models.

4. TikTok Marketing API data

Authorization and scope. Authorized operators connect to official TikTok Ads Manager accounts using TikTok's OAuth authorization flow. ZoneAds accesses only the advertiser identifiers, account settings, campaign/ad-group/ad structures, creative assets, and aggregated reporting needed to manage our direct campaigns. ZoneAds never requests or collects TikTok passwords, private messages, or unrelated user data.

Purpose. TikTok platform data is used to manage authorized advertising campaigns, upload and organize owned video creatives, monitor real-time spend thresholds, and review platform performance metrics under direct operator supervision.

Tokens and security. Authorization credentials are processed server-side, encrypted in transit and at rest, and restricted strictly to authorized team members responsible for campaign execution.

Retention and deletion. API tokens are retained only while needed for an active connection. Operators may revoke authorization through TikTok controls at any time, or email privacy@zoneadsai.com to request immediate deletion of stored platform tokens. Revocation stops future API requests immediately.

5. Sharing and service providers

We share information only when strictly necessary to operate our infrastructure (such as hosting and database providers bound by confidentiality terms) or when an operator explicitly transmits an ad campaign to an advertising network. We do not sell or monetize personal data.

6. Your rights and choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of stored personal data at any time by contacting privacy@zoneadsai.com or following our account deletion instructions. Disconnecting a platform integration can be performed independently of any account data.

7. Contact us

For privacy inquiries, data deletion, or questions regarding our connected platform integrations, contact privacy@zoneadsai.com. General inquiries: support@zoneadsai.com.
